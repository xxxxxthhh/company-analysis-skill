- One-line thesis: "monopolistic gateway to U.S. compliant crypto infrastructure, transitioning from cyclical exchange to diversified financial platform"

**Revenue transformation narrative:**
- Transaction revenue ~55-60% → declining share (cyclical)
- Subscription & services ~40% → growing share (structural)

- 🏰 Strong: regulatory compliance barrier, S&P 500, ETF custodian for 9/11 ETFs
- 🏚️ Weak: no pricing power on trading fees, inherent crypto cyclicality
