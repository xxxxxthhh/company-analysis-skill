# Example Report: Salesforce (CRM)

> Shows: enterprise SaaS analysis, NRR/Rule of 40/CAC payback metrics, margin expansion thesis, management track record analysis

## Structural Highlights

### Executive Summary Pattern
- One-line thesis: "Enterprise SaaS incumbent transitioning from growth-at-all-costs to margin-expansion story under activist pressure"
- Quick stats: Market Cap, P/E, Revenue Growth, FCF Yield, Rule of 40 score

### SaaS Framework Application

**Key SaaS-specific metrics in practice:**
| Metric | Data | Interpretation |
|--------|------|---------------|
| Revenue Growth | Application of SEC-derived CAGR | Show 3-year trend |
| NRR | 🔍 From earnings call / IR | >120% = excellent retention + expansion |
| Rule of 40 | Calculated: Growth% + FCF Margin% | >40% = efficient growth |
| SBC as % of Revenue | SEC-derived | SaaS red flag if >15% |
| RPO / cRPO | 🔍 From 10-K / earnings | Forward revenue visibility |
| Operating Margin trend | SEC-derived | Margin expansion narrative central to thesis |

### Activist Pressure & Management Analysis

This is a case study in management quality under external pressure:
- Activist involvement as catalyst timeline marker
- Management response metrics: headcount changes, margin targets, capital return policy
- Track record: historical guidance accuracy pre- and post-activist pressure

### Dialectical Structure

**Bull case pillars:**
1. Margin expansion from 20% to 30%+ under activist pressure
2. NRR >110% proves enterprise stickiness
3. AI product (Einstein GPT) as next growth cycle
4. Capital return (buybacks) as new management priority

**Bear case pillars:**
1. Revenue growth deceleration structural, not cyclical
2. AI-native competitors (low-code, agentic) disrupting CRM model
3. Margin expansion is one-time cost-cutting, not durable operating leverage
4. M&A integration risk (Slack, Tableau still digesting)

**Key uncertainty:** Is the margin expansion sustainable, or is it cost-cutting that masks revenue deceleration?

### Why This Example Matters
1. Enterprise SaaS with all key SaaS metrics applied (NRR, Rule of 40, RPO, SBC)
2. Management quality analysis under activist pressure — a distinct analytical angle
3. Revenue growth vs margin expansion tradeoff — the core SaaS valuation debate
4. Shows how to analyze a company in transition (growth → value profile shift)
