# Foreign Small-Cap Theme Stocks: Primary Listing vs OTC ADR/F-Shares

Use this note when analyzing non-US small caps that trade both on a home exchange and as US OTC tickers (e.g., Swedish `*.ST` plus OTC `*F`).

## Core workflow

1. **Resolve identity carefully**
   - Confirm legal name, home ticker, OTC ticker, and avoid confusing similarly themed names/tickers.
   - Explicitly state when an OTC ticker is only a secondary trading line for the same ordinary share.

2. **Use official/home-market disclosures first**
   - Prefer annual/interim reports from the company IR site or regulator/home-market mirrors.
   - If the company IR site blocks scraping, a filings mirror can be used to locate the official PDF, but cite the original document title/date when possible.

3. **Separate theme fit from stock attractiveness**
   - A company can be a real thematic fit and still be a bad risk/reward after a large move.
   - For policy/MOU-driven themes, distinguish: non-binding policy language, no committed funding, no company named, and actual customer/order conversion.

4. **Normalize valuation to home-market share count and currency**
   - Compute market cap from primary listing price × latest shares outstanding.
   - Compute simple sanity multiples (P/S or EV/Sales) against latest audited or interim sales.
   - Explicitly flag when valuation already implies successful commercialization/scale-up.

5. **Check dilution/runway**
   - For pre-profit small caps, inspect cash, operating cash flow, recent directed issues, warrants/options, and share-count growth.
   - A high stock price can become a financing window; dilution risk is part of the thesis.

6. **Trading venue judgment**
   - If the user has access to the home exchange via IBKR or similar, prefer the primary listing over OTC for liquidity/price discovery.
   - OTC lines may have wider spreads, worse execution, and lower liquidity; treat them as inferior unless the user cannot access the primary venue.

## Output pattern

For these names, use a concise conclusion-first structure:

- **Conclusion:** real theme fit or not; buy/watch/avoid at current price.
- **Why it fits:** business lines mapped to policy/industry theme.
- **But:** valuation, dilution, customer concentration, event risk.
- **Primary vs OTC:** which line to use and why.
- **What to verify next:** upcoming earnings, backlog/orders, design-win conversion, cash runway, financing risk.

## Pitfalls

- Do not call a company “zero revenue” if it has meaningful audited revenue; say “pre-profit” or “revenue too small relative to valuation.”
- Do not equate partnerships/design wins/MOUs with booked revenue or backlog.
- Do not let a strong theme override market expectations already priced in after a multi-bagger move.
- Do not ignore customer concentration for small semiconductor suppliers.
